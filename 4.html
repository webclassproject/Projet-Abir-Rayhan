Requirements & Qualifications

Are You Ready to Teach Abroad?

We are looking for passionate and dedicated teachers who want to share their knowledge and experience with students around the world. Before applying, please make sure you meet the following requirements.

1. Educational Requirements

  • A bachelor's degree in English, Education, or a related field.
  • A teaching certificate such as TEFL, TESOL, or CELTA is preferred.
  • Applicants with relevant teaching qualifications are encouraged to apply.

2. Language Skills

  • Excellent spoken and written English.
  • Good communication and presentation skills.
  • Ability to explain lessons clearly to students.

3. Professional Experience

  • Previous teaching experience is an advantage.
  • Ability to prepare lessons and classroom activities.
  • Patience, creativity, and a positive attitude.

4. Personal Qualities

  • Adaptability to different cultures and environments.
  • Strong communication and teamwork skills.
  • Responsibility and professionalism.
  • Passion for education and working with students.

5. Required Documents

  • Updated CV or Resume.
  • Copy of university degree.
  • Teaching certificates (if available).
  • Copy of passport.
  • Motivation letter.

Ready to Join Us?

If you meet these qualifications and are interested in international teaching opportunities, we would love to hear from you!
